Method & caveats

What is measured

For each district we sum pentavalent dose-1 (9.1.6) and dose-3 (9.1.8) totals from the HMIS monthly service returns. The dropout ratio is (dose1 − dose3) / dose1, clipped to 0–100%. A district is flagged high risk when its ratio sits at or above the 80th percentile of all monitored districts, and watch above the 55th.

Why a district is flagged

Each district card lists the signals that push its risk up, ranked by contribution: whether the loss is concentrated at the 2nd or 3rd dose, how rural the catchment is, month-to-month volatility, a seasonal collapse in sessions, and the multi-year direction of travel. These are computed from the same HMIS returns — not a black box.

Known limits

HMIS records doses delivered and reported, not a true birth cohort, so denominators drift with reporting quality. Districts with fewer than 100 recorded dose-1 events in the latest year are excluded. Boundary changes between 2017 and 2020 mean a few districts lack a full three-year trend.

Using this

The ledger is a triage aid: it points state and district teams — and partner NGOs — toward the catchments where a fixed amount of outreach capacity will recover the most children. Pair it with local knowledge before acting.
